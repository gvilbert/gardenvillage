Plans

Ground floor

The pavilion: kitchen on the back wall, clean island and table for eight in front of the glass wall, living around the stone fireplace. The floating stair runs along the glass to the mezzanine. Entry through the glazed vestibule on the stone side. The private block behind keeps the V18 layout. The outdoor kitchen moves under the covered terrace.

Upper floor

The stair arrives on the mezzanine lounge, open to the pavilion. The suite door opens into the bedroom, glazed onto the loggia; the bathroom and the low dressing follow. A glass door from the mezzanine leads to the open part of the roof terrace, where the outdoor stair also arrives.

Sections

Section B-B, across the glass pavilion

The roof rises 20% toward the garden: 4.40 m clear at the stone wall, 6.40 m at the glass. A 1.50 m overhang with an oak soffit shades the glass from the high summer sun; the low winter sun comes in.

Section C-C, across the private block, suite and loggia

The suite bedroom is on the high side (3.40 m clear), glazed onto the loggia. The bathroom follows, then a low dressing under the roof. The loggia box roof sits at +6.10, below the main roof.

Notes

Trade-offs

The single slope is low on the 10D side, so the upstairs is tall on the glass side and low on the other: suite bedroom 14.4 m² (was 17.2), mezzanine 28.0 m² (was 32.0), dressing as low storage.

An 8.00 × 6.40 m glass wall faces the afternoon sun: the 1.50 m overhang helps; add exterior blinds or low-e solar glass, and plan cleaning access.

Large steel-framed glass costs more per m² than stone walls.

The loggia box and the roof terrace are flat roofs: confirm with GV, and detail the waterproofing over the office.

The carport sits in the 5.00 m front setback. Garden Village has to approve it.
