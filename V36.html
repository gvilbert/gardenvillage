Plans

Ground floor

Entry through a small glazed vestibule with a coat cupboard. The helical stair (Ø 2.20 m) starts facing the room. The outdoor kitchen sits on the deck against the house, with a pass-through window to the kitchen counter; the outdoor stair climbs from the deck to the roof terrace. The annex holds the garden office (with the old house corner, glass on two sides), the guest shower room and an outdoor storage room opening onto the covered terrace. The hall leads to the office, both bedrooms and the laundry, which opens to the garden strip at the back.

Upper floor

The suite faces the terrace again: from the mezzanine you enter the dressing, which leads to the bedroom on the terrace side and the bathroom at the back. The bedroom window sits in the dormer, behind a 1.20 m planter so terrace guests stay at a distance. The roof terrace is 43.0 m², with a bar and the outdoor stair arriving at its front corner.

Sections

Section C-C, across the roof suite

Walls 4.80 m, ridge inside 6.95 m, top 7.25 m: 0.25 m under the limit. Upstairs the walls are 1.50 m at the eaves; full standing height runs 7.21 m across.
